Help improve ScoutSheet?

With your OK, ScoutSheet counts per day which features you use (modes, searches, profiles, exports, My Club), how long the app is open, its version, your OS and any errors, under a random ID.

Never sent: your save, file names, players, clubs or leagues.

Counts go to scoutsheet.fm, wait on your computer while you are offline, and are deleted after 14 months. You can change your mind anytime in Settings: turning it off also deletes what was sent.
